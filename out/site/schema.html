Capability properties

FieldTypeMeaning
ownerref · inherit · requiredPerson or team accountable for the capability: its roadmap, budget and risk decisions. Set it on a domain or group and everything beneath inherits it unless it sets its own. Name a User or Team entity by its name, id or (for a User) email.
criticalityenum · default medium · inheritHow much the business suffers when this capability is unavailable or degraded. It sets recovery targets and how closely the capability is reviewed.
  • low — Can be down for days using workarounds, with little customer or financial impact.
  • medium — Disruption hurts within a day, causing delays and manual workarounds but limited customer impact.
  • high — An outage stops core operations or causes financial, legal or customer harm within hours. Needs a recovery plan, tested failover and named on-call support.
review_cycle_daysint · default 180 · inheritDays between scheduled reviews of the capability's owner, providers and maturity.
maturityenum · default initialHow well established the way of delivering this capability is.
  • initial — Ad hoc; it depends on individuals and has no documented process.
  • pilot — Being trialled in part of the organisation.
  • defined — Documented, standard process used consistently.
  • optimized — Measured and improved continuously.
lifecycleenum · default activeWhere the capability is in its life.
  • plan — Planned but not delivered yet.
  • active — In use.
  • retire — Being phased out. Nothing active may sit beneath it.

Relation attributes

exposes

The entity delivers this capability for the rest of the organisation.

FieldTypeMeaning
coverageenum · default fullHow much of the capability this entity delivers.
  • full — Delivers the whole capability.
  • partial — Delivers only part of it; another provider is needed for the rest.

requires

The entity needs this capability to do its job. Something active must expose it.

FieldTypeMeaning
dependencyenum · default hardWhat happens to the entity when nothing provides the capability.
  • hard — It cannot operate. An unmet hard requirement is a break.
  • soft — It keeps running in a degraded way.

depends_on

The application calls or relies on another application or service at runtime.

FieldTypeMeaning
criticalityenum · default mediumHow badly the application suffers when the dependency fails.
  • low — Hardly noticed, or easily worked around.
  • medium — Some features fail; the rest keeps working.
  • high — The application stops working.
